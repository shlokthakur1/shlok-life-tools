How your vote counts

You vote for one lower house member and for five upper house members in your region. A party needs 45 of the 88 lower house seats to govern alone. Parliament of Victoria

Preferences matter this year. The ABC's analysis says One Nation may end up with the balance of power, and that the Coalition could need a formal agreement or bill-by-bill negotiation if it falls short of a majority. Roy Morgan's 26 to 29 October poll had primary votes at Coalition 29.5%, Labor 22.5%, One Nation 22% and Greens 12.5%. ABC, polling list. Greens, independents and minor parties are on the ballot too, but aren't compared here. The ABC tracker covers more parties.
