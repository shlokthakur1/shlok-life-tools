# Dubai Relocation

## Lock in the job — by 30 Nov

- [ ] Sign the offer and get the 4 Jan start date confirmed in writing
- [ ] Ask HR exactly what they need for the work visa (passport scan, photo, degree certificate) and send it
- [ ] Ask HR if your degree must be attested (DFAT, then the UAE Embassy) and start it now if so, as it can take weeks
- [ ] Ask what the company covers: flight, temporary housing, relocation allowance, health insurance
- [ ] Check your notice period, then resign and agree your last day
- [ ] Check your passport has at least 6 months left on it

## Book the big things — by 7 Dec

- [ ] Book the flight to Dubai, landing 2–3 days before 4 Jan
- [ ] Book 2–4 weeks of temporary stay near Downtown so you can view rooms in person
- [ ] Sort the Melbourne rental: tell the agent your plan and check the lease notice you have to give
- [ ] Arrange who looks after the dog, and book any handover
- [ ] Book a session with a tax adviser on Australian tax residency and the investment properties

## Money, property and admin — by 13 Dec (before Vietnam)

- [ ] Tell each property manager you're moving overseas and update your contact details
- [ ] Tell your banks you'll be living overseas and get a card with no foreign transaction fees
- [ ] Keep your Australian mobile number on a cheap plan or eSIM so 2FA codes still reach you
- [ ] Pick someone in Australia to collect mail and sign for things, and set up mail redirection
- [ ] Ask your private health fund about suspending cover while you're overseas
- [ ] Update your address and contact details for super, ATO, Medicare and insurance
- [ ] Scan passport, degree, licence and birth certificate into cloud storage
- [ ] Fill any prescriptions and check each medicine is allowed into the UAE

## Wind down Melbourne — 22 Dec to 3 Jan

- [ ] Decide what to take, store, sell or give away
- [ ] Cancel or transfer internet, power, gym and subscriptions
- [ ] Hand over at work and finish on your last day
- [ ] Move-out clean and final inspection, if you're leaving the rental
- [ ] Pack, checking the airline's baggage allowance
- [ ] Carry the originals: passport, degree, Australian licence
- [ ] Farewells

## Once you land (don't stress about these yet)

- [ ] Get a UAE SIM and a Nol card for the metro and bus
- [ ] Do the visa medical test and Emirates ID biometrics when HR books them
- [ ] Open a UAE bank account once you have your Emirates ID
- [ ] View co-living rooms in Downtown near the Silicon Oasis bus route
- [ ] Convert your Australian licence to a UAE licence, if you'll drive
